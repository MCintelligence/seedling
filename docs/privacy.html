Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026 · Applies to Seedling 1.0 for Android and iOS, by SlopLVI

Your photos stay on your device.
  • All editing, including every AI feature, runs on your phone or tablet.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or cookies.
  • SlopLVI collects no personal information and sells or shares nothing.

Who we are

Seedling is a photo editor and sticker maker made by SlopLVI, an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Seedling app.

Your photos

Everything Seedling does to your photos, including the AI features (Magic Eraser, Background Changer, Portrait Blur, Face Retouch, Auto Enhance and Upscale), runs on your own device. Your photos are never uploaded to SlopLVI or to anyone else.

Photo access

You choose photos to edit with your device's own photo picker, which shares only the photos you pick. Seedling does not read the rest of your photo library.

When you save an image, Seedling adds it to your photo library. On iOS it asks for permission to add photos only. On Android 10 and newer no permission is needed; on Android 9 and older it asks for storage permission so it can save the image.

What is stored, and where

Your creations (stickers and photos you save in the app), any AI model you download and your settings are kept in Seedling's own storage on your device. SlopLVI cannot see them. They stay until you delete them in Settings or uninstall Seedling. If your device's own backup is turned on, your operating system may include this data in your personal backup; that backup is handled by Google or Apple under their policies, not by SlopLVI.

Internet use

Seedling's AI models are included in the app, so it works without an internet connection. If a future version offers an optional larger model and you choose to download it in Settings, it is downloaded from SlopLVI's file host. Like any web request, the host can see standard technical details such as your IP address. Seedling adds no personal information to the request, and nothing else is downloaded or sent.

What Seedling does not do

No accounts. No advertising. No analytics, tracking or cookies. No selling or sharing of data. Seedling does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts. Vibration is used for feedback and can be turned off in Settings.

Purchases

Seedling is free, with no in-app purchases, so it never asks for payment details.

Children

Seedling collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because SlopLVI holds no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete everything Seedling stores on your device at any time in Settings → Storage → Clear all, or by uninstalling the app. This policy is written to meet Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and similar laws elsewhere.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and the app will show the updated policy in Settings.
